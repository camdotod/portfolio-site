About Me

Hi! My name is Camryn (they/them) and I'm a multidisciplinary designer located in Toronto, Ontario.

I studied Industrial Design at Carleton University where I learned how the design process can be applied to create product, services, and systems that meet people’s needs. Since graduating I’ve had experience working in commercial product design, app development, and community projects.

What is a multidisciplinary designer?

“Multidisciplinary designer” is a term that some designers use to better represent their range of skills in multiple areas of design, such as graphic design, product design, web design, and user experience design.

While working in each of these areas requires industry-specific knowledge, the foundational understanding of how to use the design process to solve problems is the same.

It is my belief that sometimes we must question whether the end-product (whether that's an app, product, or service) is suited to the problem at hand, so for me, the title multidisciplinary designer reflects my desire to learn about and practice design broadly.
